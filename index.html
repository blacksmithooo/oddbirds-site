Oddbirds

A flock of odd birds.

Oddbirds is an animated comedy series about a flock of handmade, one-of-a-kind birds, told in short wordless films.

Oddbirds Studio is the small tool we use to send our own finished films to our own TikTok account as drafts, so we can review them before posting. It is not offered to other users.
